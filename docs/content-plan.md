- 6N (99.9999%) is an intended silica development target, not a certified or achieved result. Silica and elemental silicon are explicitly distinguished.
- No DPR, named leadership roster, verified capacities, product certifications, completion dates, offtake agreements or support for 3,000/7,000 jobs was established. Those claims are withheld.

- Product ordering must remain Silicon / Bio-Silica, SAF, CBG. Preserve the existing list setup but bypass content randomization for those ordered lists; maintain the footer's existing row calculations.
