# Rivanta research ledger — agricultural residue, silica and silicon

Prepared 18 November 2026. Research only; no company capacities, certifications, facility status, partners or employment totals verified. No project code edited.

## 1. Agricultural residue resource: use a dated baseline

**Supported website wording:** India has a substantial agricultural-residue resource. Assessing the sustainably available surplus is a necessary first step in planning industrial biomass supply.

**Evidence:** Sardar Swaran Singh National Institute of Bio-Energy, National Biomass Atlas, official MNRE-institution resource. It reports approximately 230 million metric tonnes/year of surplus agricultural residues for **2017–18**. Its underlying ASCI/MNRE assessment gives 228.52 million tonnes surplus for the triennium ending 2015–18.

**Sources:**
- https://nibe.res.in/english/biomass-atlas.php
- https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3716e1b8c6cd17b771da77391355749f3/uploads/2023/05/2023053179.pdf

**Caveat:** This is a historical resource estimate, not a fresh 2026 survey, and not all estimated surplus is economically collectable or environmentally appropriate to remove from fields. Do not imply it is Rivanta's contracted feedstock.

## 2. Collection and logistics matter

**Supported website wording:** Seasonal collection windows, moisture, contamination, storage and transport all affect biomass quality and delivered cost. Organized collection and residue aggregators can help build dependable supply chains.

**Evidence:** Official SSS-NIBE Jaiv Urja Mitra training framework includes residue-availability calendars, farmer interaction, sampling, contamination/moisture limits, baling, storage ventilation, route planning, transport and fire/rotting risks.

**Date/scope:** File reference 348-26/27/2025-ADMIN; published in 2026 according to search metadata. Training framework rather than a quantitative market study.

**Source:** https://nibe.res.in/pdf/EOI-Jaiv_Urja_Mitra_SSS-NIBE.pdf

**Caveat:** Do not imply every residue is interchangeable, or promise farmers fixed prices or guaranteed income. Paddy straw, rice husk, wheat straw and sugarcane residues require different handling and processing assessments.

## 3. Residue burning and North Indian air quality

**Supported website wording:** Burning residues to prepare fields for the next crop contributes to seasonal air pollution in North India, including Delhi-NCR. Practical alternatives need collection systems, appropriate machinery and viable end uses.

**Evidence/date:** Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change parliamentary answer, **16 March 2026**, describes residue burning as one significant contributor worsening winter air quality and covers in-situ management, ex-situ utilization, machinery support and rural entrepreneurs.

**Sources:**
- https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2240644&lang=1&reg=1
- https://sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/187/AU3733_oVrk4O.pdf?source=pqals

**Caveat:** Do not call residue burning the sole cause of Delhi pollution, claim an unqualified fixed percentage, or promise Rivanta will end burning. A historical ICAR annual report search excerpt supports short sowing windows, but its large PDF timed out during verification; omit a numeric 15–20-day claim unless another fully inspected primary source is used.

## 4. Rice-husk silica has established industrial applications

**Supported website wording:** Silica-rich rice husk ash can be processed into precipitated silica for applications including tyre reinforcement, paints and coatings, and cement-related materials, subject to product specification and customer qualification.

**Evidence:** CSIR-IICT technology profile, Process Development of Precipitated Silica from Rice Husk Ash. The live page provides no publication date and lists patent application IN202311064373.

**Source:** https://techindiacsir.anusandhan.net/online/process-development-of-precipitated-silica-from-rice-husk-ash-T-1592-tech.htm

**Caveat:** Independent technology precedent, not proof of a Rivanta licence, technology partnership, achieved grade, operating plant or environmental performance. Do not transfer the profile's process-specific cost/emissions advantages to an unselected Rivanta process.

## 5. Silica and elemental silicon must remain distinct

**Supported website wording:** Bio-silica is silicon dioxide (SiO2). Elemental silicon requires a separate reduction step, with additional purification for demanding downstream applications.

**Evidence:** Royal Society of Chemistry's official silicon element reference explains silica/silicates, elemental silicon reduction, and separate high-purity manufacturing. NIH PubChem's silica record identifies the SiO2 compound; last modified **14 November 2026** when checked.

**Sources:**
- https://periodic-table.rsc.org/element/14/Silicon
- https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/Silicon-dioxide

**Caveat:** Extracting rice-husk silica does not automatically make silicon metal, semiconductor material or solar-grade silicon. Avoid using “silicon” and “silica” interchangeably in titles or captions.

## 6. Biogenic silica extraction requires process and quality validation

**Supported website wording:** Feedstock preparation, extraction and purification conditions determine silica quality. Rivanta's 6N (99.9999%) purity specification should be presented solely as an intended target requiring validation.

**Evidence/date:** Park et al., **26 September 2023**, primary pilot-scale study. A continuous alkali-hydrothermal and milling process produced amorphous spherical silica at 99.1 wt% purity under its experimental conditions. The researchers specifically discuss operating conditions, yield/throughput trade-offs and energy requirements.

**Source:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40538-023-00479-4

**Caveat:** The paper is not evidence of 6N achievement, semiconductor qualification or Rivanta performance. The 6N target comes from the user-provided company brief; it is not externally verified as achieved or certified. Additional silicon processing is an independent research/engineering route, as illustrated by https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0958305X231204991 (primary study, first published **8 October 2023**).

## 7. Imported industrial silica demonstrates a market, not automatic displacement

**Supported website wording:** India's imports of silicon dioxide show an established industrial market supplied by several countries, including China. Domestic biomass-derived materials would need consistent specifications, competitive economics and customer qualification.

**Evidence/scope:** World Bank WITS display of UN Comtrade, **2024**, HS **281122** silicon dioxide: imports US$105.23 million; China US$26.34 million. This also includes multiple grades and forms.

**Source:** https://wits.worldbank.org/trade/comtrade/en/country/IND/year/2024/tradeflow/Imports/partner/ALL/product/281122

**Caveat:** Values are gross trade, not domestic consumption, market size or the share India depends on imports. They do not measure demand for 6N purity. Prefer qualitative wording on the site; retain numbers internally if the existing layout cannot display product code and year clearly.

## 8. Silicon-metal trade is a separate supply-chain context

**Supported website wording:** India's silicon-material supply chains include substantial international sourcing. Developing domestic capability is a strategic opportunity, subject to grade, economics and technical qualification.

**Evidence/scope:** World Bank WITS/UN Comtrade, **2024**, HS **280469** silicon containing <99.99% silicon: imports US$185.16 million; China US$166.92 million (about 90.1% by value, arithmetic inference).

**Source:** https://wits.worldbank.org/trade/comtrade/en/country/IND/year/2024/tradeflow/Imports/partner/ALL/product/280469

**Caveat:** This category is explicitly below 99.99% silicon. It must not be used to claim China supplies 90% of India's semiconductor-grade silicon, all silica, polysilicon or total silicon-related imports. No national consumption denominator was established. The WITS HS280461 high-purity series appeared unusually small and was not used for market-size claims.
